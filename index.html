How to Play

  • Drag a piece from the tray onto the board. You can also tap a piece, then tap the board cell where its top-left block should go.
  • Pieces cannot be rotated.
  • Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Rows and columns clear at the same time.
  • You get three new pieces after all three are placed.
  • The game ends when none of the remaining pieces fit.

Scoring (Classic)

5 points per block placed, plus 80 × lines² for clears (1 line = 80, 2 lines = 320, 3 lines = 720).

Blast mode

  • Pieces are picked to clear lines often. Every game starts on an empty board.
  • Each clear adds to your streak. The streak survives up to 3 moves without a clear; the dots under it show how many moves you have left.
  • Clears are multiplied by your streak: 1 line = 20, 2 = 60, 3 = 120, 4 = 200, then × streak. Blocks placed are worth 1 each.
  • Colour Wipe: clear the last blocks of a colour for 50 × streak per colour. Board Clear pays 300 × streak.
  • Blast keeps its own best score. Switch modes in Settings → Game.

Keyboard

1–3 select a piece · arrows aim · Enter/Space place · Esc cancel
